This arrangement reimagines Mendelssohn’s _Rondo Capriccioso_, Op. 14, as a virtuosic showpiece for violin duo. The principal sources include the original version for piano solo, as well as an arrangement for violin and piano by Abram Yampolsky (1890–1956). I give my gratitude to violinists Hina and Fiona Khuong-Huu—to whom this arrangement is dedicated—as well as Geoffrey John Davies and Thomas Mathias, who contributed essential editorial feedback.
