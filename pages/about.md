                    <p>Composer <b>Kian Ravaei</b> (b. 1999) takes tone painting to a new level, synthesizing diverse inspirations into evocative musical portraits. Whether he is composing a string quartet inspired by wonders of the natural world, electronic music that evokes the pulsating energy of late-night dance clubs, or a symphonic poem that draws from the Iranian music of his ancestral heritage, he takes listeners on a spellbinding tour of humanity’s most deeply felt emotions.</p>
                    <p>The 2025-26 season sees a variety of performances, including the New York premiere of Ravaei’s <a href="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/works/gulistan-clarinet/"><i>Gulistan</i></a> at the <a href="https://www.chambermusicsociety.org/" target="blank">Chamber Music Society of Lincoln Center</a>, as well as two new string quartets for the <a href="https://www.abeoquartet.com/" target="blank">Abeo Quartet</a> and <a href="https://www.sheffieldchamberplayers.org/" target="blank">Sheffield Chamber Players</a>. He has been named a <a href="https://classeek.com/news/meet-the-2025-26-ambassador-programme-artists%2C68" target="blank">2025-26 Classeek Ambassador Programme Artist</a>, and will partake in a one-year career enhancement program alongside six of the world’s most promising classical musicians.</p>
                    <p>From Carnegie Hall to Pierre Boulez Saal, musicians such as Grammy Award winner <a href="https://www.fleurbarron.com/" target="blank">Fleur Barron</a>, Performance Today Classical Woman of the Year <a href="http://www.laradownes.com/" target="blank">Lara Downes</a>, and New York Philharmonic clarinetist <a href="https://www.anthonymcgill.com/" target="blank">Anthony McGill</a> have brought Ravaei’s music to global stages. The <a href="https://asq4.com/">Alexander String Quartet</a> capped their 44-year career with a farewell tour that featured Ravaei’s seven-movement string quartet <a href="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/works/the-little-things/"><i>The Little Things</i></a>. His works have been commissioned by prominent chamber music organizations—among them <a href="https://www.seattlechambermusic.org/" target="_blank">Seattle Chamber Music Society</a> and <a href="https://cmnw.org/" t arget="blank">Chamber Music Northwest</a>—as well as the <a href="https://www.americancomposers.org/" target="blank">American Composers Orchestra</a>, where he is currently a resident <a href="https://www.americancomposers.org/earshot-composer-incubator/residencies" target="blank">CoLABoratory Fellow</a>.</p>
                    <p>Notable honors include a <a href="http://www.coplandhouse.org/composers/cultivate/" target="_blank">Copland House CULTIVATE Fellowship</a>—during which he participated in an emerging composers institute at Aaron Copland’s National Historic Landmark home—as well as commissioning grants from <a href="https://chambermusicamerica.org/news/announcing-the-2024-classical-commissioning-grantees/" target="_blank">Chamber Music America</a>, <a href="https://newmusicusa.org/program/new-music-creator-fund/" target="_blank">New Music USA</a>, and the <a href="https://barlow.byu.edu/commission-recipients" target="_blank">Barlow Endowment</a>. Ravaei’s rapidly expanding catalog has earned him first prize awards in the New York Youth Symphony First Music Chamber Music Competition, the Foundation for Modern Music Robert Avalon Competition, and the Zodiac International Music Competition.</p>
                    <p>Born to Iranian immigrants, Ravaei maintains close ties to the Iranian community in his hometown of Los Angeles. Many of his works combine the ornamented melodies of Iranian classical music with the colorful harmonies of Western classical music. Mezzo-soprano <a href="https://www.fleurbarron.com/" target="blank">Fleur Barron</a> and pianist <a href="https://www.kunallahiry.com/" target="blank">Kunal Lahiry</a> commissioned Ravaei to compose a Persian-language setting of the feminist Iranian poet Forough Farrokhzad for their U.S. tour, culminating in a sold-out Carnegie Hall recital. A passionate speaker, Ravaei was a featured lecturer at the <a href="https://farhang.org/workshops-lectures/composing-identity-notes-from-an-iranian-american-composer" target="_blank">UCLA Iranian Music Lecture Series</a>, where he discussed his multicultural upbringing and its deep-seated influence on his music.</p>
                    <p>Just days into the COVID-19 lockdown, Ravaei began a daily ritual of playing a Bach chorale at the piano and composing an original chorale in response. What started as a way to ground himself during a period of emotional turbulence blossomed into an artistic reawakening. Over the course of one year and three hundred sixty-five chorales, Ravaei cultivated a “rich harmonic idiom” (<a href="https://washingtonclassicalreview.com/2025/03/15/mezzo-soprano-barron-closes-vocal-arts-season-with-a-multicultural-smorgasbord/" target="blank">Washington Classical Review</a>) rooted in a centuries-long tradition.</p>
                    <p>As part of his residencies at chamber music festivals across the Western hemisphere, Ravaei engages with local audiences through educational presentations, musical performances, and community events. He was a resident composer at <a href="https://cmnw.org/" t arget="blank">Chamber Music Northwest</a> through their <a href="https://cmnw.org/community-education/past-proteges" target="blank">Protégé Project</a>, and later became the inaugural composer-in-residence at <a href="https://skmozart.com/" target="blank">Sunkiss’d Mozart</a>. Through a trailblazing partnership between the <a href="https://www.wyomingchambermusic.org/" target="blank">Wyoming International Chamber Music Festival</a> and the <a href="https://www.tenbymusicfestival.com/" target="blank">Tenby International Music Festival</a>, Ravaei serves as composer-in-residence of both festivals, helping to foster musical dialogue between the United States and United Kingdom.</p>
                    <p>Millions of classical radio listeners have heard Ravaei’s music on the airwaves, from New York’s WQXR to Los Angeles’s KUSC. As part of <a href="https://assets.ctfassets.net/8usdt07j8s00/1lzO9NVveJjEydpvA7F5oH/18faa36f619662e79b87dce7e7e074df/The_2024_Classical_California_Ultimate_Playlist.pdf" target="blank">Classical California’s 2024 Ultimate Playlist</a>, the nation’s largest public radio listenership ranked Ravaei’s piece <a href="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/works/latif/"><i>Latif</i></a> in 26th place—sandwiched between Elgar’s <i>Enigma Variations</i> and Mendelssohn’s <i>Violin Concerto</i>—making him the only living composer in the top 30. His music has been featured on award-winning radio programs such as <a href="https://www.yourclassical.org/episode/2022/09/29/kian-ravaei" target="_blank">APM’s Performance Today</a> and <a href="https://www.wnycstudios.org/podcasts/newsounds/episodes/4968-lara-downes-and-friends-studio" target="blank">WNYC’s New Sounds</a>, as well as his personally curated streaming station for <a href="https://classicalmusicindy.org/local-classical-kian-ravaei/" target="_blank">Classical Music Indy</a>.</p>
                    <p>With numerous commercial recordings, Ravaei has earned critical acclaim from outlets including <a href="https://www.gramophone.co.uk/review/talla-rouge-shapes-in-collective-space" target="blank">Gramophone</a>, <a href="https://daily.bandcamp.com/best-contemporary-classical/the-best-contemporary-classical-music-on-bandcamp-september-2024" target="blank">Bandcamp Daily</a>, and <a href="https://icareifyoulisten.com/2024/09/talla-rouge-explore-the-violas-dynamism-and-virtuosity-on-debut-album/" target="blank">I CARE IF YOU LISTEN</a>. His compositions appear on albums such as <a href="http://laradownes.com" target="blank">Lara Downes</a>’ <a href="http://www.laradownes.com/this-land-album" target="blank"><i>This Land</i></a>—a poignant reflection on American identity—and <a href="https://www.tallarouge.com/" target="blank">Tallā Rouge</a>’s genre-bending debut <a href="https://www.brightshiny.ninja/shapes-in-collective-space" target="blank"><i>Shapes in Collective Space</i></a>. Fans of electronic dance music will hear Ravaei’s orchestration in the official orchestral arrangement of <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wooli" target="_blank">Wooli</a> & <a href="https://g.co/kgs/JDeesm" target="_blank">Codeko's</a> "<a href="https://ophelia.ffm.to/crazyorchestral" target="blank">Crazy (feat. Casey Cook)</a>,” which has garnered over two hundred thousand views across streaming platforms.</p>
                    <p>Choreographers have tapped into Ravaei’s music as a source of inspiration, transforming his vivid sound worlds into dance. They include <a href="https://www.marlaphelan.com/" target="blank">Marla Phelan</a>—whose innovative fusion of dance and video projections set to Ravaei’s immersive electronic score premiered at the <a href="https://www.juilliard.edu/news/176511/juilliards-center-creative-technology-presents-inaugural-future-stages-festival" target="blank">Juilliard Future Stages Festival</a>—and <a href="https://www.therosinboxproject.com/biographies/carly-topazio" target="blank">Carly Topazio</a>, who captivated audiences with her choreography to Ravaei’s <i>Family Photos</i> during a joint performance by <a href="https://artofelan.org" target="_blank">Art of Elan</a> and <a href="https://www.therosinboxproject.com/" target="blank">The Rosin Box Project</a>. Most recently, Ravaei and choreographer <a href="https://www.aliveandwellproductions.org/" target="blank">Annie Kahane</a> completed a three-year project to combine Persian and Jewish musical and dance traditions, which debuted at the <a href="https://www.sfiaf.org/" target="blank">San Francisco International Arts Festival</a>.</p>
                    <p>Inspired by the generosity of his own teachers—celebrated composers such as <a href="https://www.valeriecoleman.com" target="_blank">Valerie Coleman</a>, <a href="https://www.johncorigliano.com/" target="_blank">John Corigliano</a>, and <a href="https://www.richard-danielpour.com/" target="_blank">Richard Danielpour</a>—Ravaei pays forward his musical training by empowering others to embrace their creativity. He recently launched the <a href="https://www.tenbymusicfestival.com/wales-wyoming-workshops/" target="blank">Wales-Wyoming Workshops Composer Fellowship</a>, a tuition-free program for early-career composers from the U.S. and U.K. to gain transatlantic exposure through performance and recording opportunities. In previous years, Ravaei taught composition to historically underserved students as a <a href="https://www.laco.org/people/kian-ravaei/" target="blank">Composer Teaching Artist Fellow</a> for the <a href="https://laco.org/" target="_blank">Los Angeles Chamber Orchestra</a>, and also held a teaching position at the <a href="https://jacobsacademy.indiana.edu/descriptions/composition-academy.html" target="blank">Indiana University Jacobs Composition Academy</a>, where he mentored composers aged 17 to 70.</p>
                    <p>Ravaei’s own musical journey has led him eastward from the <a href="https://schoolofmusic.ucla.edu" target="_blank">UCLA Herb Alpert School of Music</a> and the <a href="https://schoolofmusic.ucla.edu" target="_blank">Indiana University Jacobs School of Music</a> to the heart of New York City, where he is currently a C.V. Starr Doctoral Fellow at <a href="https://www.juilliard.edu" target="_blank">The Juilliard School</a>.</p><br>
